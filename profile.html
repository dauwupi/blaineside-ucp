How two-step verification works

Worth two minutes before you turn it on — especially the part about losing your phone.

Which apps can I use?

Any of them. This uses the standard every authenticator speaks, so Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, Bitwarden, Aegis and Ente Auth all work identically. We don't have an app of our own and never will — that's the point of a standard.

If you already use a password manager, put the code in there: it syncs across your devices, so a lost phone stops being a crisis.

Does this protect my forum account too?

Yes. The forums don't have their own password — signing in there hands you to the UCP, and the UCP is what asks for your code. So turning it on here covers forum.blaineside.com in the same move.

The forum software has its own separate 2FA setting in its profile area. You don't need it, and turning it on means entering two codes for one sign-in.

What happens if I lose my phone?

You use a recovery code. At the sign-in prompt, click "Lost your phone? Use a recovery code" and enter one of the ten you saved. It signs you in exactly like an app code does, then gets marked used.

Once you're in, turn two-step off and set it up again on your new phone.

If you've lost the phone and the recovery codes, there is no self-service way back in — that is what makes it worth having. Open a ticket and a Founder will clear it after verifying who you are. Expect that to take a day or two, and expect to be asked questions only you can answer.

What exactly are recovery codes?

Ten one-time passwords, generated when you switch two-step on. Each is accepted once at the sign-in prompt instead of a code from your app, and is then dead forever.

They're shown once and stored scrambled, so nobody — staff included — can look yours up afterwards. If you lose the list, generate a new set from this page: it replaces all ten and the old ones stop working immediately.

Can I get past it by resetting my password?

No, deliberately. A password reset only proves someone can read your email — if that were enough to strip two-step off, then anyone who got into your inbox would own your account, and the second step would be decoration.

So a reset changes your password and leaves two-step exactly as it was. Your recovery codes are the way through, which is why saving them matters.

I think someone has my password. What now?

In this order: change your password from the Settings tab — that signs out every other device on its own — then turn on two-step verification if it isn't already, then check Where you're signed in below for anything you don't recognise.

If you see a sign-in that wasn't you, tell staff in Discord with the date and time. We keep sign-in records and can check them against what you report.

How your record works

What affects my standing?

A warning or a kick turns your standing yellow for 30 days from the day it was issued. Go 30 days without picking one up and it goes back to normal on its own.

Nothing ever leaves your record. The entry stays there permanently — it just stops affecting your standing.

Bans work differently: a temporary one ends on its own date, and a permanent one is permanent.

Who can see this?

You and staff, nobody else. It isn't on your forum profile, it doesn't show in game, and other players can't look it up.

I think an entry is wrong. What do I do?

Appeal it from Reports & Appeals and give the date and time off the row. It goes to someone other than the admin who issued it.

If it's overturned we mark it void — it stays on your record, but it stops counting.

Why can't I delete my UCP while I have entries?

Deleting the account would take the record with it. If that worked, anyone facing a ban could wipe their history and come straight back as a new player.
