Record filters — five options

Each one is live: click through the states. They all carry the same information — four choices and a count each — so the difference is entirely in how much of the page's attention they take and how well they scale.

01

Segmented bar

Spans the card, equal segments, gold wash and a solid underline on the active one. The loudest option — impossible to miss and it anchors the table beneath it. Costs a full-width band of the card, and it stops working past about five types.

02

Underlined tabs

Labels on a rule, counts in a muted weight beside them, gold underline on the active one. The quietest option — reads as a view switcher belonging to the table rather than a control competing with it. Weakest at catching the eye of someone who doesn't know it's there.

03

Type-coloured chips

The selected chip takes its own type's colour — amber for warnings, blue for kicks, red for bans — matching the badges in the Type column exactly. The clearest link between the filter and the rows it produces, and the state is obvious from across the room. Puts more colour on the page than anything else here.

04

Table toolbar

A dropdown and a search field on one line, with the result count pushed right. The only option that still works at twenty types, and search is genuinely useful on a record with two years of entries. Costs you the at-a-glance counts, and one more click to change type.

Counter tiles
